# **Compact - By Zak Heath**

This sleek yet playful website is the digital home of rising fashion influencer Zak Heath, who boasts 80K Instagram followers and over 500K on TikTok. 

The site features exclusive content that offers a behind-the-scenes look into the fashion world, with a strong focus on original interviews and first-hand conversations with prominent figures across the industry. From in-depth articles and podcast episodes to personal essays, the content captures the voice and vision of its creator—offering both style and substance to readers. Whether you're here for insider insights or a closer look at the influencer's own fashion journey, this site is where influence meets authenticity.

### **Stack**
This site is built in Next.js with Typescript, hosted in Vercel. This site also makes extensive use of libraries like React Three Fiber and GSAP to create an engaging and fun vibe that aligns with the nature of its content.
